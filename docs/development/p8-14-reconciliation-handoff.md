## 2. Sequence and ownership

Call [Identity.open_reconciliation/1, review_reconciliation/1 and apply_reconciliation/1](../../lib/new_you/identity.ex). [Reconciliation](../../lib/new_you/identity/reconciliation.ex) validates request keys, loads the case and uses private owning Ash actions. All involved Accounts, including actor, candidates and recorded reviewer, lock in UUID order before case and authority work. Both scoped grants lock in sorted ID order after the Account union; revocation also takes its subject Account first. This serialises distinct cases that overlap candidates, including reversed pairs.

Opening records an explicit proposed canonical Account and detection basis; it performs no merge. Approval requires current authenticated controls for both candidate Accounts, current scoped Support grants for each candidate Account, purpose-bound MFA step-up and owner review. Review stores references, Account security/reconciliation versions, reviewer/grant versions and timestamps. It retains no bearer, password, MFA secret or copied foreign dossier.

Apply performs a preflight transaction, commits independent [Audit.admit_reconciliation/1](../../lib/new_you/audit.ex), then opens the source transaction and repeats current authority, version, control and owner checks. Case apply, losing Account closure/PMR retirement, losing active Session revocation, canonical Account reconciliation-version advancement and linked E2 obligation commit together. Provider I/O is absent from the transaction.

[Communications.review_identity_reconciliation/2](../../lib/new_you/communications.ex) confirms that message histories retain original Account associations. Its delivery executor continues to ask Identity for current source eligibility. [Audit.review_identity_reconciliation/2](../../lib/new_you/audit.ex) preserves append-only evidence associations. These responses are fixed owner policies, not per-record inspection. Neither owner requests a remap or asynchronous consequence for the currently implemented verification/magic-login intents and append-only Audit records. Integration tests exercise real pending intents for both Accounts and existing registration evidence. Retired-source magic-login and verification intents close without a provider attempt on execution and remain bound to their original source; retries stay closed. Survivor records retain their original association. A changed configured Domain inventory blocks approval/apply until explicit owner review is implemented. Commercial, health and professional records are not assumed covered by these responses.

## 4. Interface contracts

`open_reconciliation/1` requires `credentials`, `grant_id`, `canonical_grant_id`, `candidate_id`, `canonical_id`, `operation_id` and bounded `detection_basis`; `compensates_id` is optional. Candidate is the proposed non-survivor. Canonical selection is explicit human intent, never an age, PMR or demographic algorithm. Equal candidates, ineligible Accounts and conflicting operation bindings fail closed.

`review_reconciliation/1` requires `credentials`, `grant_id`, `canonical_grant_id`, `case_id`, `expected_version`, `controls` and `decision`. Approval requires exactly two current control credentials. Controls must resolve to the two case candidates, and review keeps only their Account/Session references and check time. Apply rejects checks older than five minutes, revoked/expired Sessions, missing/revoked user tokens, changed Account versions and stale reviewer assurance and both recorded grants. The five-minute limit uses microsecond precision. Material rejection/conflict appends an E2 obligation atomically with its source history entry.

`apply_reconciliation/1` requires `credentials`, `grant_id`, `canonical_grant_id`, `case_id`, `expected_version` and `operation_id`. Success returns `{:ok, ReconciliationCase}`. Unavailable, denied, stale, conflicting or malformed requests collapse to `{:error, :reconciliation_unavailable}`. Same-operation replay additionally binds case, actor, both grants/versions, Session, expected version and reason to the original E1/apply. Replays require current privileged authority; they do not rerun retired-account control checks or repeat effects.

These are owner interfaces without new HTTP routes. A Session or PMR reference alone confers no reconciliation capability. [The authentication reachability test](../../test/new_you_web/security/authentication_reachability_test.exs) retains the password-only strategy and exact existing route inventory and checks that the new Account actions are private.

## 5. Lifecycle, failure and recovery

The implemented graph is `detected`, then `candidate`, then `review`, followed by `approved`, `rejected` or `conflict`. Conflict may return to review. An approved case may return to review or be rejected under current pair-scoped authority. Rejection remains available under current pair-scoped authority when an overlapping winner has already retired a candidate. It changes only case provenance and cannot apply the stale decision. Both paths clear the current approval and append its full restricted evidence as `superseded_review` in immutable history. Re-review requires fresh controls and owner checks; stale versions cannot reuse the discarded approval. Only approved may become applied. Rejected and applied cases are immutable terminal records. Every transition increments the case version and appends provenance.

Compensation creates a new case linked through `compensates_id` to an applied decision whose current canonical Account is the new non-survivor. It requires the normal control, owner review, privilege, E1 and apply checks. The bounded correction chooses a current open canonical Account with its own globally fresh PMR. It never reopens a terminal losing Account, resurrects its PMR, rewrites the original decision or transfers foreign provenance. More general recovery/restoration is outside this implementation.

Pre-admission failure has no Identity effect. An E1 admission survives later source failure. A caught source failure records a linked failed E2 outcome if that evidence transaction is available. A failed operation cannot later be silently reported as succeeded. Process termination after admission leaves the unapplied case reconstructible and requires a freshly authorised retry. Termination after source commit leaves the applied decision and E2 obligation intact; replay returns the committed case and existing Audit scheduling can materialise evidence without replaying Identity.

If both the source transaction and failed-result evidence transaction are unavailable, E1 remains an admission with unresolved outcome. The interface returns unavailable and does not fabricate a failed or successful merge. Operator reconciliation/retry uses durable case and current authority. There is no background job that silently reapplies privileged Identity work.

## 6. Persistence and consistency

[The generated schema migration](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108150417_well15_reconciliation.exs) creates case foreign keys, unique detection/apply-operation indexes and state/decision checks; adds Account reconciliation dimensions; and extends case-bound Audit evidence and Support scope constraints. Existing Accounts backfill as active PMR, no canonical link and reconciliation version zero.

[Guard migration](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108150423_well15_reconciliation_guards.exs) adds the Account canonical FK, append-only case history and legal transition checks, permanent retired-PMR protection and exact E1/E2 reconciliation binding. [Lookup indexes](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108150512_well15_reconciliation_indexes.exs) cover each candidate and status/update ordering. [TRUNCATE protection](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108151200_well15_preserve_case_history.exs) closes the statement-level deletion bypass. [Material review evidence](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108151726_well15_review_evidence.exs) adds the selected E2 type.

[Approval recovery](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108170000_well15_approval_recovery.exs) extends the legal graph and requires the old approval in appended history before clearing it. [Pair-scoped admission](../../priv/repo/migrations/20261108170100_well15_pair_scope.exs) requires canonical grant ID/version for reconciliation E1 while prohibiting those fields on other assertion kinds. The constraint is added with `validate: false` so immutable E1 admissions from earlier draft builds survive unchanged. PostgreSQL enforces the strict pair shape on every new insert/update. A regression proof retains a legacy-shaped row and rejects a new incomplete row. Legacy admissions cannot satisfy the new exact binding and cannot be used to apply or replay. Its rollback refuses to discard populated scope evidence.

The losing Account keeps its original ID and PMR bytes, becomes `closed_terminal`, changes its credential to revoked and retains a canonical link. Existing permanent PMR reservation triggers remain in force. There is no implemented DeviceAssurance Resource or trusted-device authentication path in this runtime. Durable Session revocation, closed Account/credential guards and current token presence checks enforce the implemented access boundary. The retained user token cannot bypass the revoked Session or closed Account; device lifecycle is not claimed as implemented. Rollback refuses to discard new case/retirement/evidence truth and requires governed disposition. The guards protect ordinary writes; they do not claim protection against a database owner disabling triggers.

## 8. Extension guide and cold-start audit

Start at the three Identity interfaces, then follow `authority/3`, `review_evidence/4`, `applicable/4` and `commit_or_replay/5` in Reconciliation. Read the Resource action guards and schema/trigger migrations before changing lifecycle or persistence. Use the focused tests to reproduce each failure window. The author followed these links against the recorded source; independent Master Gate still must verify the exact final head.

A new affected Domain must expose its own bounded review and any required durable owner command before the inventory guard can change. Add commands only for actual required consequences, with durable recoverable intent and the target owner's idempotency contract. Do not let Identity rewrite foreign records, treat Audit or job state as canonical identity, infer matching from demographics, reuse old PMRs, add production grant issuance or expose private Resource actions through a controller.
