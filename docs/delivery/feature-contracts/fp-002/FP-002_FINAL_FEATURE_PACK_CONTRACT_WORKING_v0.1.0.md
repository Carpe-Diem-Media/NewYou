## Accepted purchase and owning boundaries

Commerce resolves an approved version and governs the accepted payer and recipient relationship. Preserve the accepted currency, price, component and discount allocations and rule version. The bundle remains unavailable until Commerce approves and discloses the exact allocation and it reconciles to R549. Refund attribution uses the accepted snapshot. Product §21R.1 and DEC-299 fix this behaviour and leave the split to versioned Commerce configuration.

Entitlements owns unused Assessment-credit truth. A held or available unused credit prevents a second standalone Assessment or bundle purchase, while an independently eligible Plan-only purchase remains possible. The annual restriction uses prior successful digital Assessment delivery. Admission arbitrates distinct intents for the canonical recipient, including while the competing attempt can still collect or its paid entitlement consequence is outstanding. Required source history that cannot be established blocks affected admission. DEC-303/313 govern the credit and later delivery boundary.

## Payment and entitlement outcomes

Commerce records an accepted obligation separately from its attempts and evidence. The attempt and reconciliation reference are durable before external dispatch. Commerce accepts only appropriately qualified evidence bound to the known integration, environment, reference, actual amount and currency, lossless transaction identity and accepted payment relationship. Browser return may trigger reconciliation. Ambiguous mutation outcomes preserve the original reference and block replacement until irreversible nonpayability and any in-flight collection are resolved.

Commerce commits the verified payment with a recoverable consequence. Entitlements separately applies one right per eligible source/component. The initial standalone Plan right is `held_unconsumed`. This confirms the purchased right for the authorised recipient. Payment and grant alone do not establish a Safety pathway, successful Plan delivery, visibility of delivered Plan content or consumption. Product §21R.1 and DEC-299 govern later fulfilment or closeout. Retry, concurrent application and restart preserve the same right and its provenance.

E1b must recover a known pending attempt through a durable single-reference reconciliation path, even when neither browser nor webhook returns. Scheduled work must rediscover or resume that pending reference after process restart. Its bounded test proves one eventual Commerce payment and one right from qualifying verification. E1c supplies later cross-object scan completeness under overlapping pages, checkpoints, outage and rate limits. E1b evidence must pass with E1c absent.

## Correction and disclosure

Duplicate evidence of one collection has no additional monetary effect. Verified genuine excess for one obligation creates one exact make-whole claim against the lawful collection amount, owed to the authoritative payer. Independently accepted obligations stay distinct. Commerce reconciles unsolicited or ambiguous monetary outcomes and attributes components only from accepted allocation. Entitlements applies an authorised source-scoped suspension, restoration or end and preserves unrelated rights. Corrections preserve lawful history and cannot restore access after deletion. Privacy, Audit, Communications and operator disclosures remain purpose- and scope-bound. Audit evidence never replays a business mutation. These rules follow DEC-300/314 and Domain §§6.10–6.11.

## Proof and exit

The required architecture demonstrates qualified evidence to one durable payment and one source-specific consequence under duplicate delivery, concurrent application and restart. Preserve actual transport, signed ingress, individual and cross-object recovery, and monetary correction proof at their declared depths. The separate classification gate selects a proof vehicle after 7C approval. See the [classification candidate](../../proof/fp-002/FP-002_ARCHITECTURAL_PROOF_CLASSIFICATION_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md) and [proof design candidate](../../proof/fp-002/FP-002_TRACER_BULLET_DESIGN_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md).

Otherwise S1 requires its separately satisfied architectural proof entry gate for ordinary slice development.

Exit requires the three eligible offer versions, governed bundle disclosure, reconciled payment and entitlement outcomes, unused-credit admission, visible corrections and applicable provider, recovery and capacity proof.
