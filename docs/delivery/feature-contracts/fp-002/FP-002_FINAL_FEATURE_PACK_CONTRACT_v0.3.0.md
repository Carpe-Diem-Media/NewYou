## Outcome and purchase admission

Use approved, versioned Commerce offers for the Assessment, Plan and bundle for once-off South African ZAR checkout. Launch amounts are governed Commerce configuration selected before launch; the R249, R399, R549 and R99 values used in development are synthetic fixtures and are not launch prices. Commerce owns price and acceptance, identifies the intended obligation and records the authorised payer and canonical recipient relationship. The bundle discount must be allocated by an approved, deterministic Commerce offer version and disclosed before checkout. Its complete snapshot records currency, accepted total, allocation version, component amounts and discounts in minor units. Incomplete allocation, stale offer or missing eligibility evidence prevents affected acceptance.

Entitlements owns unused Assessment credit. An available or held unused ordinary credit blocks a new Assessment or bundle purchase. Plan-only stays independently eligible. The annual interval uses prior successful paid digital Assessment delivery from the owning boundary. Commerce must arbitrate distinct attempts for the same canonical recipient while a competing attempt can still collect or a paid consequence remains outstanding. If the required source history is unavailable, refuse the affected admission. Temperament owns the delivery evidence and has no purchase or credit write authority. The same Assessment credit holds, consumes or releases under §21R.6 and DEC-313. Downstream Assessment delivery remains outside this pack.

## Payment evidence and recovery

Keep the purchase, each provider attempt, receipt, Commerce payment, right, correction, merchant settlement and reconciliation work distinct. Persist the attempt and its unique reconciliation reference before dispatch. Apply provider I/O outside short owner transactions. Only Commerce qualifies evidence for the known integration, environment, attempt and reference, actual customer amount and currency, lossless transaction identity and accepted payment relationship. Verify authenticity where applicable, durably receive signed ingress before acknowledging it and reconcile through Commerce. Browser return may trigger reconciliation. Provider fees, a requested amount, provider customer identity and settlement state cannot redefine the accepted charge, Identity authority or paid outcome. Keys remain server-side. Hosted card handling and minimised metadata keep raw card credentials and sensitive participant content outside the payment path.

Unknown provider values, mismatches, timeouts and ambiguous mutation acknowledgements stay unrecognised or unresolved for reconciliation. Block blind retries, including retries hidden by client, middleware or proxy. Reuse the original reference and only resume a checkout capability with qualifying evidence bound to that attempt. Replace an attempt only after irreversible nonpayability and resolution of any in-flight collection. Read retries are bounded and rate-limit-aware. Exhausted work remains visible and recoverable.

Commerce records verified payment and its mandatory durable consequence together. Entitlements applies one right per eligible source/component through its owning interface after rechecking current authority. Stale observations, concurrent apply, lost acknowledgement and restart converge without a second effect. An authorised recipient can read the initial standalone Plan right in `held_unconsumed` after the grant commits. The right stays held through applicable incomplete information, technical failure or review. Safety & Eligibility owns pathway decisions and Plans owns successful delivery evidence. Only governed fulfilment consumes the right. Governed terminal closeout uses the accepted Plan allocation, and the applicable general-wellness choice and closeout follow §21T.4. A grant alone establishes neither consumption nor access to delivered Plan content.

E1b must rediscover or resume a known pending reference on a durable schedule across restart while browser return and webhook delivery are absent. Temporary failure leaves the attempt pending. Later qualified verification must yield one payment and one source-specific consequence. Prove E1b independently of E1c. E1c demonstrates complete bounded cross-object replay, including overlapping and late pages, checkpoint cuts, exhaustion, rate limits and outage. An incomplete page cannot advance the durable completeness boundary.

## Corrections and disclosure

Duplicate evidence of one genuine collection has no additional monetary effect. For genuine excess on one obligation, Commerce records and satisfies one exact make-whole claim for the authoritative payer relationship. Independently accepted obligations stay distinct. Ambiguous correction and competing refund/dispute paths reconcile before another mutation. Provider execution failure, including insufficient merchant balance, leaves owed money and the work to recover it visible. Only the immutable accepted allocation identifies a component. Unattributable partial evidence leaves a visible adjudication case and cannot terminate a guessed right.

Entitlements applies only the authorised source-specific suspension, restoration or end, with current policy checks. Preserve unrelated valid rights and lawful transaction and delivery history. Deletion prevents access resurrection. Finance may act only through scoped Commerce authority. Support and the payer receive only the permitted commercial view. Identity controls actor and recipient authority, Privacy governs purpose and retention, and Audit captures selected evidence independently of business execution. External dispute submissions use the minimum necessary commercial evidence. Sensitive participant material requires governed escalation. Communications delivery and Analytics measures cannot confer payment, access or correction authority. Apply the separately governed copy and notice rules.

## Proof and exit

P1, E1a–c and E2a–c carry `HIGH` risk with `financial` and `entitlement` consequences at the relevant boundaries. Apply the [Engineering Standards v1.1.0 proof menu](../../../governance/supporting/ENGINEERING_STANDARDS_v1.1.0.md#tests-review-and-proof) to each exact Ready scope. S1–S3 also require HIGH review where they enforce authoritative admission, access or correction. Record applicable cases and justified exclusions for independent reviewer agreement.

- P1 must prove acceptance and evidence rejection, owner and recipient authorisation, independent PostgreSQL races, apply-once and replays, pre-commit rollback and fresh recovery at both post-commit cuts. Prove the held-right boundary, TEST-only evidence exclusion from production, and positive, stale, conflict and restart cases for Identity reconciliation.
- E1 must prove actual mutation counts and ambiguity guards, exact binding, signed receipt and acknowledgement boundaries, negative and unknown observations, redaction, single-reference repair and separate paginated completeness. Apply each case at its declared stage.
- E2 must prove exact excess and claimant, accepted component attribution after repricing, concurrent corrections and reordering, unresolved or failed refund recovery, scoped dispute effects, preserved independent rights, restricted evidence and no resurrection after deletion. Preserve the distinct synthetic, provider and actual-stack obligations.
- Each relevant scope must substantiate migration safety, redaction and leakage protection, security/privacy and authority review, recovery observability and focused sensitivity to the consequential guard. Use stage-specific fault and capacity proof where applicable. Resources, indexes, job and transaction mechanics remain JIT within the owner boundaries.

The [proof classification](../../proof/fp-002/FP-002_ARCHITECTURAL_PROOF_CLASSIFICATION_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md), [P1 proof design](../../proof/fp-002/FP-002_TRACER_BULLET_DESIGN_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md) and [implementation plan](../../execution/fp-002/FP-002_IMPLEMENTATION_AND_TICKET_PLAN_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md) remain separate decisions. Demonstrate one authoritative version for each active price, disclosed and reconciled bundle allocation, distinguishable pending and verified outcomes, one valid right per eligible component, retry and reordering integrity, visible refunds and reconciliation, and the unused-credit purchase interlock. Validate bounded latency, contention and backlog recovery under callback bursts and provider interruption. Close the applicable OQ-004 stages with accepted evidence or explicit governed non-applicability. Paid release separately requires the operating and abuse-control gates, channel and live operations evidence, eligible content and applicable legal, privacy, security and paid-pilot approvals. This contract grants no execution, ticket publication or release authority.
