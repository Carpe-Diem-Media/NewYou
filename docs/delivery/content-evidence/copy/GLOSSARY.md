## Brand and audience rules

- The public brand name is **not yet locked**. Participant-facing copy must use `{{platform_name}}`; never hardcode `NewYou` or another future brand into canonical UI strings. The repository/project may still be referred to as `NewYou` when discussing technical provenance.
- Participant-facing copy normally addresses the person as **you / jy-jou** instead of calling her “the participant / die deelnemer”.
- Keep business concepts distinct: mailing-list contact ≠ account holder ≠ purchaser ≠ recipient ≠ participant ≠ member/customer ≠ entitlement. Do not translate them into one catch-all “lid”.
- Afrikaans must be South African Afrikaans. Avoid Dutch forms and `ij` spellings; use forms such as `vergelyking`, not `vergelijking`.

## Legal UX terminology clarification

- English governing artifact title: **Privacy Notice** (preserve the source document title).
- Afrikaans participant-facing term: **Privaatheidsbeleid**. Do not use `Privaatheidskennisgewing` in current participant UI candidates.
- `entitlement` in participant-facing Afrikaans should normally be rendered as **toegang** / **toegangsreg** only where the underlying meaning requires the more formal right; the cookie experimentation candidate therefore uses **toegangsreëls**, not `aanspraakreëls`.
