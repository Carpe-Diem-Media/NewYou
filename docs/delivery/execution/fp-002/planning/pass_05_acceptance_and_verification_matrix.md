### 5.1 Shared test setup and observation contract

- Create **one synthetic verified Identity Account** acting as both payer and recipient; choose the single approved standalone R399 Plan version with **39,900 ZAR minor units**. Persist one uniquely bound purchase, a known pending attempt/reference and a **TEST-only internal synthetic** evidence source. Interact with **real owning Commerce and Entitlements Ash interfaces and real PostgreSQL**; no mocked owner state. No Paystack network/SDK/keys/provider operations.
- For every scenario report **fresh before/after durable observations** through owner-accessible queries or safe scoped DB verification: accepted purchase identity, payment count and identity, committed grant-consequence count and state, Entitlements Plan right count and current state, correct/foreign recipient read decision, delivery count and consumption count (both zero in P1).
- Counts describe **one accepted commercial obligation and one source/component**, not a universal cardinality limit over all purchases. The original accepted purchase may persist after a **payment-application** rollback; P1-03 requires **zero committed payment and zero consequence**, not erasure of previously accepted intent.
- Give scenarios deterministic identifiers, expected error classes, timestamps/commit cuts and redacted log/telemetry pointers; no participant data, private attempts, production credentials or auth capabilities in CI/Plane/public evidence.
- Separate **acceptance replay**, **financial evidence replay**, **grant-consequence replay**, **concurrent independent writers**, and **fresh process recovery**; one scenario cannot silently stand in for another.
