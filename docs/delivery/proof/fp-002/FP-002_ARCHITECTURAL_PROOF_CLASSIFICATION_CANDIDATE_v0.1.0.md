## Proof gap

FLOW-02 establishes the ownership and failure rules. FP-001 supplies reusable PostgreSQL, owning-action and restart proof patterns. The offline Hosted Redirect model demonstrates fixture behaviour. The remaining architectural claim is that real NewYou Commerce commits one verified payment and durably causes one Entitlements right across separate owning interfaces, concurrent delivery and process loss. No existing proof exercises this boundary, so `REUSE_EXISTING_PROOF` cannot cover it.

## Decision boundary

Record the formal decision only after 7C approval. Approval of the classification selects the vehicle. The [proof design](FP-002_TRACER_BULLET_DESIGN_CANDIDATE_v0.1.0.md) needs separate accountable-human approval. P1 can certify a bounded synthetic sub-proof. Complete FP-002 Phase 8 certification requires independent assessment of all applicable architectural proof claims, including separate provider evidence where required. FP-002 exit and release have their own gates. Classification grants no execution, publication or release authority.
