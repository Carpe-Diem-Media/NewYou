## 5. Our privacy approach

We aim to:

- process personal information lawfully and reasonably;
- collect information for specific, explicit purposes;
- collect only what is reasonably needed for those purposes;
- collect sensitive information progressively, when a relevant Service actually needs it, rather than building a large profile “just in case”;
- distinguish authoritative information from derived, inferred or provider-supplied evidence;
- keep personal information reasonably accurate where accuracy matters;
- restrict access according to role and need;
- retain identifiable information only as long as there is a valid reason to do so;
- protect information using safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity and risk;
- provide meaningful transparency about material processing;
- preserve applicable access, correction, objection, withdrawal, deletion and complaint rights; and
- apply heightened care to health and other special personal information.

Information collected for one purpose does not automatically become available for every other purpose.

## 38. Account closure and full deletion

1. full deletion is requested;
2. normal Account access is revoked and optional processing is stopped as governed;
3. a **14-day cancellation window** applies;
4. after the window, if the request has not been validly cancelled, each owning Domain performs the applicable deletion, irreversible anonymisation, suppression or restricted lawful-retention consequence;
5. deletion completion is recorded and there is no Account recovery or reconstruction path.

Before deletion execution, NewYou should explain what will be lost and, where feasible and lawful, provide an opportunity to export information you are entitled to retain.

During the 14-day window, a valid cancellation stops the pending deletion process through the governed recovery route.

Full deletion should trigger an orchestrated process across authoritative records and applicable processors rather than merely hide the Account.

Each owning Domain should delete, destroy, de-identify, suppress, restrict or lawfully retain the information it owns according to the approved lifecycle.

Downstream indexes, caches, analytics links/projections, embeddings and search records must not silently survive as independent authority after authoritative information is deleted.

Lawfully retained finance, legal, audit, security, suppression or professional records may survive where required, but must be narrowly access- and purpose-restricted.

Retained evidence must not be used to recreate deleted Assessments, plans, journals, health history, entitlements or the deleted participant experience.

A later new Account does not automatically reconnect to information destroyed through completed full deletion.
