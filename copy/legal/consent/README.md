# Consent and legal interactions

Purpose-specific consent only; no bundled catch-all permission. Terms acceptance and Privacy acceptance remain distinct durable facts; Privacy Notice presentation is a separate disclosure. Product Law leaves the participant-facing control count open. Age confirmation, marketing permission, health/special-PI authorisation, practitioner sharing and early-performance instructions also remain distinct facts where applicable. Privacy acceptance does not grant consent to every processing purpose.
